OUR STORY

From a garage sketch to a global stride

STRIDE began in 2011 when two runners, frustrated by shoes that looked fast but felt like bricks, decided to build their own. What started as hand-cut prototypes tested on dawn laps around the block has become footwear trusted by marathon winners, trail rebels, and everyday movers in 38 countries.


We don't chase trends. We chase the feeling of a perfect run — that effortless, floating moment where shoe and foot become one. Every STRIDE is engineered to deliver that feeling, step after step.
